Killed the duplicate stats. The hero strip and the four cards below it showed the same four numbers twice. Now one strip — and each figure carries a delta vs the previous period, which is what makes a number an insight rather than a readout.

Insights answers what Briefing can't. Briefing = who needs you today. Insights = how the roster is trending and how clients compare. Hence the weekly adherence trend and the distribution — neither exists anywhere else in the app.

The table now compares. Inline bars in the adherence and execution cells, so your eye ranks the roster instead of reading 13 numbers. Sortable by any column. Clients with no programme say "— no programme" rather than a row of bare dashes.

Period selector (4wk / 12wk / 6mo) top-right — Insights is inherently about a window, and there wasn't one.

Saturation kept: solid blue tab and range control with glow, coloured stat numbers, glowing trend and distribution bars, coloured avatars.
