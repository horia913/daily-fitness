------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Updates profiles.leaderboard_visibility
  2. hidden  → deleteClientLeaderboardEntriesAndRecalc (delete all rows for
               client, then recalc each affected coach partition so ranks
               close gaps)

               not immediately on flipping back to public.
