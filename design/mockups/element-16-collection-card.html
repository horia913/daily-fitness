Element 16 — Collection card

The governed pattern for roster / collection pages — the coach's list of Programs, Plans, and Clients. Each entity is its own dark item-card in a single-column stack, framed by a colored left rail and a soft right glow in the item's hue, carrying enough detail to act without opening it.

Law amendment

Boxes are earned — one card per screen, everything else open rows.
Boxes are earned. Surfaces exist on: the hero card (one per screen), the collection card (roster pages — Programs / Plans / Clients), the tab bar, button fills, chips, avatar. In-context sub-lists inside a single-item view — exercises within a workout, meals within a plan — stay open rows (Element 06). Roster pages of top-level entities use collection cards (Element 16).

Rules
  • Where it applies: roster / collection pages of top-level entities — Programs, Plans, Clients. Not for sub-lists inside one item.
  • Left rail + right glow = the item's hue. Programs cycle the group hues (A/B/C/D); Clients may map hue to status/score.
  • Structure strip is optional and content-specific — segmented bar with per-segment labels. Omit when the type has no such structure.
  • Phase colors are a fixed intensity scale, not decorative: light / base → moderate → hard / peak, and deload / recovery stands apart. The bar reads as training stress at a glance. (Mapping each real block to a level — by block type, name heuristic, or order — is pinned at build time.)
  • Right stat is the type's headline metric — assigned count (Programs), or score ring / adherence (Clients). One number, glanceable.
  • Effects budget holds: the glow is ambient (not the CTA glow); the page's one CTA glow still lives on the header's primary action.
  • Full-width on desktop; collapses to a stacked layout under 1024px (right zone drops below the body).
