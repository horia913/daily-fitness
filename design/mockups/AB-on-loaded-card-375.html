The dashed line is the bottom of an iPhone SE screen — everything below it needs a scroll.

A — the log row and your first set row clear the fold. On a loaded exercise you can see the target, the cue, and log without scrolling.
B — the extra ~55px pushes the log row to the fold itself and the set rows below it. Still perfectly usable, just a scroll to see what you logged.

Honestly: B is the nicer thing to tap, and if this were the sparse card I'd say take it. The cost only shows up on a maxed exercise like this one — and that's the case you asked me to design for.

A middle option, if you want it: B's stacked fields with a compact ✓ beside the reps row instead of a full-width "Log set" button — recovers about half the height and keeps the wide, easy targets.
